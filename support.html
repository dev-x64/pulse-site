Common questions

Where is Pulse?

Pulse lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon. If you use a menu bar manager, check its hidden area. Hold Command and drag the Pulse indicator to an available position in the menu bar. macOS controls how much space is available.

How do I open settings or quit?

Click the Pulse indicator, then the gear to open Settings. The power button quits Pulse. You can also right-click the menu bar indicator for Settings and Quit.

Why is the temperature unavailable?

Pulse shows the hottest available CPU sensor in °C or °F. Sensor availability varies by Mac model and macOS version. If a reading cannot be obtained, Pulse shows a dash; it does not substitute an estimated temperature.

What does thermal state mean?

macOS reports system thermal pressure separately from sensor temperature. Pulse presents it as Normal, Warm, High or Critical. It is an assessment of system thermal conditions, not a temperature in degrees.

Why does the chart start empty?

Pulse builds a rolling history during use. Wait up to a minute for the complete 60-second window. History is held in memory and is cleared when Pulse quits.

How do I launch Pulse at login?

Install Pulse in Applications, then enable Launch at login in Pulse Settings. If macOS asks for approval, check System Settings → General → Login Items.

Which versions of macOS are supported?

Pulse requires macOS 13 or later. The optional native Liquid Glass panel requires macOS 26 or later and respects Reduce Transparency. English and Russian are available in Settings. Temperature sensor availability depends on your hardware and macOS version.

Does Pulse upload my system data?

No. System monitoring runs locally without accounts, ads or analytics. Read the privacy policy for details about the app, support email and this website.
